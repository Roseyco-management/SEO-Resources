## What is E-E-A-T?

**E-E-A-T** stands for:
- **E**xperience
- **E**xpertise
- **A**uthoritativeness
- **T**rustworthiness

Added by Google in December 2022, the extra "E" for Experience emphasizes first-hand, real-world experience with topics you write about.

### Google's Perspective

E-E-A-T is NOT a direct ranking factor but rather a **framework Google's Quality Raters use** to evaluate content quality. However, the signals that demonstrate E-E-A-T ARE ranking factors.

**Think of it as**:
- E-E-A-T = The goal
- Author credentials, citations, reviews, original content = The ranking signals

## The Four Pillars

### 1. Experience ⭐ (Added 2022)

**Definition**: Have you actually done what you're writing about?

**What Google Looks For**:
- First-hand product usage
- Real-world testing and results
- Personal anecdotes and insights
- Original photos/videos
- Unique data or research

**Examples**:

✅ **Strong Experience Signals**:
```
Product Review:
"I tested this vacuum cleaner for 3 months in my home.
Here are photos of before/after results on my carpet.
Battery lasted an average of 47 minutes across 15 cleaning sessions."
[Original photos included]
```

❌ **Weak Experience Signals**:
```
Product Review:
"This vacuum cleaner is highly rated. It has good suction
and long battery life. Many customers recommend it."
[Generic stock photos]
```

**How to Demonstrate**:
- Include original photos/videos
- Share test results with data
- Describe your specific use case
- Mention timeline of usage
- Show before/after comparisons
- Cite personal outcomes

### 2. Expertise ⭐⭐

**Definition**: Do you have demonstrable knowledge/credentials in this field?

**What Google Looks For**:
- Relevant credentials or certifications
- Years of experience in field
- Published works or research
- Speaking engagements
- Awards or recognition
- Educational background

**Examples**:

✅ **Strong Expertise Signals**:
```
Author Bio:
"Dr. Sarah Johnson, MD, Board-Certified Cardiologist with 15 years
of clinical experience. Published 23 peer-reviewed papers on heart
health. Regular contributor to Medical Journal of Cardiology."
```

❌ **Weak Expertise Signals**:
```
Author Bio:
"Health enthusiast passionate about heart health."
```

**How to Demonstrate**:
- Detailed author bios with credentials
- Link to professional profiles (LinkedIn, ResearchGate)
- Mention certifications/degrees
- Cite your published works
- Show years of experience
- Display awards/recognition

### 3. Authoritativeness ⭐⭐⭐

**Definition**: Are you recognized as a go-to source by others in your field?

**What Google Looks For**:
- External mentions and citations
- Quality backlinks from authority sites
- Brand searches
- Social proof (followers, engagement)
- Media coverage
- Industry recognition

**Examples**:

✅ **Strong Authority Signals**:
- Featured in major publications (Forbes, NYT)
- Cited by universities or government sites
- Invited to speak at industry conferences
- High-quality backlinks from .edu, .gov, industry leaders
- Brand mentioned on competitor/review sites

❌ **Weak Authority Signals**:
- No external mentions
- Only backlinks from your own sites
- No industry recognition
- No third-party validation

**How to Demonstrate**:
- Build quality backlinks (guest posts, PR, partnerships)
- Get mentioned in industry publications
- Pursue speaking opportunities
- Participate in podcasts/interviews
- Create linkable research/data
- Engage in industry communities

### 4. Trustworthiness ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Most Important)

**Definition**: Can users trust you with their money, health, safety, or information?

**What Google Looks For**:
- HTTPS security
- Clear contact information
- Privacy policy and terms
- Transparent business practices
- Legitimate reviews
- Error-free, factual content
- No deceptive practices
- Professional site quality

**Examples**:

✅ **Strong Trust Signals**:
```
Site Features:
- Valid SSL certificate (HTTPS)
- Complete "About Us" page with real team photos
- Phone number, email, physical address
- Comprehensive privacy policy
- Third-party reviews (Google, Trustpilot)
- Editorial standards/correction policy
- Clear author attribution
- Professional design, no intrusive ads
```

❌ **Weak Trust Signals**:
```
Site Issues:
- HTTP (no SSL)
- No contact information
- No about page
- Fake/purchased reviews
- Spelling errors, outdated info
- Misleading headlines (clickbait)
- Poor design, excessive ads
```

**How to Demonstrate**:
- Implement HTTPS (required minimum)
- Complete contact page (email, phone, address)
- Professional "About Us" page
- Privacy policy and terms of service
- Legitimate customer reviews
- Editorial standards page
- Clear author attribution
- Regular content updates
- Professional site design
- Transparent business practices

## Implementation Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

**Trust Signals (Must-Haves)**:

- [ ] **HTTPS enabled** site-wide
- [ ] **Contact page** with:
  - Email address
  - Phone number (if applicable)
  - Physical address (if business)
  - Contact form
- [ ] **About Us page** featuring:
  - Company/site history
  - Mission and values
  - Team members with photos
  - Credentials/achievements
- [ ] **Privacy Policy** (use generator if needed)
- [ ] **Terms of Service** (consult lawyer for business)
- [ ] **Editorial Standards** page explaining:
  - Content creation process
  - Fact-checking procedures
  - Correction policy
  - Author vetting process

### Phase 2: Author Authority (Weeks 3-4)

**Author Profile Setup**:

- [ ] Create **author profile pages** for all writers
- [ ] Include for each author:
  - Professional photo
  - Detailed bio (100-200 words)
  - Credentials/certifications
  - Years of experience
  - Published works
  - Social media links
  - Email contact
- [ ] Implement **author schema markup**:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Person",
  "name": "Jane Smith",
  "jobTitle": "Senior SEO Strategist",
  "description": "Jane has 12 years of experience in SEO...",
  "url": "https://yoursite.com/authors/jane-smith/",
  "sameAs": [
    "https://linkedin.com/in/janesmith",
    "https://twitter.com/janesmith"
  ],
  "affiliation": {
    "@type": "Organization",
    "name": "Your Company"
  }
}
```

- [ ] Add **author attribution** to all content
- [ ] Link author names to profile pages

### Phase 3: Content Experience (Weeks 5-6)

**Demonstrate First-Hand Experience**:

- [ ] **Add original content elements**:
  - Original photos (not stock images)
  - Custom screenshots
  - Video demonstrations
  - Personal case studies
  - Test results with data
  - Before/after comparisons

- [ ] **Update existing content** to include:
  - Personal insights
  - Timeline of experience ("I used this for 6 months...")
  - Specific outcomes/results
  - Unique perspectives

**Example Template**:
```markdown
## My Experience with [Product/Topic]

**Background**: I started using [product] in [month/year] because [reason].

**Usage**: Over the past [time period], I used it for [specific use case].
Here's what I learned:

**Results**:
- Metric 1: [specific data]
- Metric 2: [specific data]
- Outcome: [what happened]

[Original photo/screenshot]

**Key Insight**: [Unique observation from experience]
```

### Phase 4: Expertise Signals (Weeks 7-8)

**Build Credibility**:

- [ ] **Add credentials prominently**:
  - Author bylines: "By Dr. Jane Smith, PhD in Nutrition"
  - About sections mention qualifications
  - Certifications displayed with logos

- [ ] **Cite sources** for claims:
  - Link to reputable sources
  - Reference studies/research
  - Quote industry experts

- [ ] **Show track record**:
  - "Our team has helped 500+ companies..."
  - "With 15 years in the industry..."
  - "Published in [Major Publication]"

- [ ] **Display awards/recognition**:
  - Industry awards
  - Certifications (Google Partner, etc.)
  - Press mentions

### Phase 5: Authority Building (Ongoing)

**External Validation**:

- [ ] **Guest posting** on authoritative sites
  - Target: 1-2 guest posts per month
  - Focus on quality over quantity
  - Author bio links back to your site

- [ ] **Build quality backlinks**:
  - Original research/data (linkable assets)
  - Expert roundups
  - Partnerships with industry sites
  - Press releases for newsworthy content

- [ ] **Get mentioned**:
  - Participate in industry surveys
  - Contribute expert quotes
  - Appear on podcasts
  - Speak at virtual events

- [ ] **Engage in communities**:
  - Answer questions on Reddit, Quora (thoughtfully)
  - Participate in industry forums
  - Helpful, non-promotional contributions

## Technical E-E-A-T Signals

### Schema Markup (Critical)

**Author Schema**:
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Article",
  "author": {
    "@type": "Person",
    "name": "Jane Smith",
    "url": "https://yoursite.com/authors/jane-smith/",
    "jobTitle": "SEO Specialist",
    "affiliation": {
      "@type": "Organization",
      "name": "Your Company"
    }
  },
  "publisher": {
    "@type": "Organization",
    "name": "Your Company",
    "logo": {
      "@type": "ImageObject",
      "url": "https://yoursite.com/logo.png"
    }
  },
  "datePublished": "2026-01-21",
  "dateModified": "2026-01-21",
  "headline": "Article Title"
}
```

**Organization Schema** (About Page):
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "Your Company Name",
  "url": "https://yoursite.com",
  "logo": "https://yoursite.com/logo.png",
  "description": "Company description...",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "123 Main St",
    "addressLocality": "City",
    "addressRegion": "State",
    "postalCode": "12345",
    "addressCountry": "US"
  },
  "contactPoint": {
    "@type": "ContactPoint",
    "telephone": "+1-555-555-5555",
    "contactType": "Customer Service",
    "email": "contact@yoursite.com"
  },
  "sameAs": [
    "https://facebook.com/yourcompany",
    "https://twitter.com/yourcompany",
    "https://linkedin.com/company/yourcompany"
  ]
}
```

**Review Schema** (For Products/Services):
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Product",
  "name": "Product Name",
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.5",
    "reviewCount": "127"
  },
  "review": [
    {
      "@type": "Review",
      "reviewRating": {
        "@type": "Rating",
        "ratingValue": "5"
      },
      "author": {
        "@type": "Person",
        "name": "Customer Name"
      },
      "reviewBody": "Review text..."
    }
  ]
}
```

## Measurement & Monitoring

### E-E-A-T Health Metrics

**Monthly Tracking**:

1. **Brand Authority**:
   - Branded search volume (track in GSC)
   - Brand mentions (Google Alerts, Ahrefs Content Explorer)
   - Domain authority trend

2. **Content Performance**:
   - Human-written vs AI content traffic comparison
   - Bounce rate trends
   - Time on page
   - Pages per session

3. **External Signals**:
   - New backlinks (quality over quantity)
   - Citation/mention count
   - Review count and rating

4. **AI Citations**:
   - Appearances in AI Overviews
   - ChatGPT/Perplexity citations (manual checks)

5. **Algorithm Stability**:
   - Ranking volatility score
   - Impact of core updates (less = better E-E-A-T)

### Tools for Monitoring

**From Catalog**:
```bash
# Crawl site for E-E-A-T issues
cd repos/seo/seonaut
# Check for: HTTPS, meta tags, author attribution

# Analyze authority distribution
cd repos/seo/crawl4ai
# Map internal linking and author pages
```

**External Tools**:
- Google Search Console (brand queries, impressions)
- Ahrefs (backlinks, mentions, content explorer)
- Semrush (authority score, brand monitoring)
- Google Alerts (brand mentions)
- Trustpilot / Google Reviews (review management)

### Red Flags to Address

❌ **Immediate Issues**:
- No HTTPS
- No contact information
- No author attribution
- Stock photos only
- Outdated content (2+ years old)
- Spelling/grammar errors
- Broken links

❌ **Medium-Priority Issues**:
- Thin author bios
- No external citations
- No original images/media
- Missing schema markup
- No reviews/testimonials
- Generic content

❌ **Long-Term Concerns**:
- Low backlink growth
- No brand mentions
- Declining brand searches
- High ranking volatility

## Industry-Specific E-E-A-T

### Healthcare & Medical

**Requirements**:
- Board-certified physicians as authors/reviewers
- Medical credentials prominently displayed
- Citations to peer-reviewed studies
- Clear medical disclaimers
- Regular content review schedule

**Schema**:
- Medical entity markup
- Organization: HealthcareOrganization
- Person: Physician with medicalSpecialty

### Finance & Legal

**Requirements**:
- Licensed professionals (CFP, CPA, JD)
- Clear disclosures and disclaimers
- Accurate, current information
- Citations to authoritative sources (SEC, IRS)
- Professional affiliations displayed

**Schema**:
- Financial service markup
- Person: credentials in jobTitle
- Organization: licenses/accreditations

### E-commerce

**Requirements**:
- Legitimate product reviews
- Clear return/refund policies
- Secure checkout (SSL)
- Contact information prominent
- Business registration info
- Trust badges (BBB, verified reviews)

**Schema**:
- Product with AggregateRating
- Organization with address
- Review markup from real customers
